# Swipe

## Read first
1. `content/swipe.md`: the anatomy, structures, swipe log, my posts, gaps.
2. `content/humor.md`: humor posts go here.
3. `content/design-content.md`: design posts go here.

## Whose post is it?
If the screenshot shows the name **Amarachi Mbanta**, it's mine. Any other name is someone else's post that moved. Never mix them up: theirs are the benchmark, mine get compared against them.

## Log a screenshot
Use for new images in `content/swipe/others/`, `content/swipe/mine/`, `content/humor/swipe/`, `content/humor/mine/`, or one I attach in chat.
1. **Sort it** by name (mine or theirs) and by type:
   - funny post: `content/humor.md`, images in `content/humor/{mine,swipe}/` (follow `/humor-post` Mode A)
   - design post (about design, UX, product, a design process or a design opinion): `content/design-content.md`, images in `content/design-swipe/{mine,others}/`
   - an **X post** (X layout: @handle, views count, reposts icon): `content/x.md` swipe log, images in `content/x-swipe/{mine,others}/`
   - anything else: `content/swipe.md`, images in `content/swipe/{mine,others}/`
2. If I attached it in chat, save it to the right folder as `YYYY-MM-DD-<author>-<topic>.png` (others) or `YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.png` (mine). Use the post's age in the screenshot to work out the date. If you can't save it, log the file as `(chat, not saved)` and tell me.
3. Open the image and transcribe the first line exactly.
4. Score it against the 9 parts of the **Anatomy** in `content/swipe.md`. Note which parts it hits. For design posts, also give the **value score** (0 to 3) from `content/design-content.md` and say where the business angle is, or that there isn't one.
5. Name its **structure**. If it's a new shape, add it under *Structures that work* with the step-by-step outline and a **design version to try** (a template for my design work, with blanks where my real details go; never fill blanks with invented facts).
6. Counts (reactions, comments, reposts, age) only if visible. Note the comment-to-reaction ratio: above ~20% means people replied with their own story.
7. Write *Why it worked* in plain sentences. Be specific: quote the lines that do the work.
8. Add the row to *Swipe log* (others) or *My posts* (mine).

## Compare: what's missing in mine
Run whenever a new screenshot of mine is logged, or when I ask.
1. Line up my posts against the swipe log: anatomy scores, hook style, length, structure, visual, comment ratio.
2. Write the **top 3 gaps**, most important first. For each: what the winners do, what my post did instead (quote it), and the exact fix for my next post.
3. Replace the *What's missing in mine* section (or *Mine vs theirs* in `content/design-content.md` for design posts) with this. Keep it short and blunt. For design posts, always say whether my post brought business value as clearly as theirs did.
4. Once there are 5+ rows total, update *Patterns across the winners*.

All writing here follows `content/human-writing.md` too.
