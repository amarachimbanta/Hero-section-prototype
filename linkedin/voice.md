## Tone
- Plain, confident, specific. Talk like a designer explaining work to a smart friend.
- Show the work → explain the decision → share the lesson.
- TODO: 3 words that describe how I want to sound (e.g. "curious, direct, generous")

## Rules
- First line is the hook: under ~12 words, no "I'm excited to share".
- Short paragraphs (1–2 lines). White space is fine.
- One idea per post.
- Concrete over abstract: name the element, the number, the trade-off.
- End with a question or a clear takeaway, not "Thoughts?" on its own.
- Max 3 hashtags, at the bottom. Emojis: TODO (none / sparing / yes).
- Length: 120–250 words unless it's a carousel caption.

## Words I avoid
game-changer, unlock, leverage, delve, elevate, "in today's fast-paced world", TODO

## Recurring post formats
1. **Build log** – what I made today and one decision behind it.
2. **Before → after** – a design change and why it's better.
3. **Teardown** – what a well-known product does well (or doesn't).
4. **Principle** – one design rule, proven with my own work.
5. **Process** – how I use Claude / Figma / code in my workflow.
