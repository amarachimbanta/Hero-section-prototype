# Sound human: no AI tells

Every post must pass this file. `content/check-post.sh <file>` flags the banned punctuation, words and phrases automatically; the patterns section is checked by reading.

Add new tells to the lists below any time. One per line, lowercase, starting with `- `.

## Banned punctuation
- Em dash (—) and en dash (–). Use a full stop, a comma, or "and". For ranges write "5 to 8".
- Arrows (→ ⇒ ➡️) in the post text.
- Semicolons. Split into two sentences.
- Emoji bullets (✅ 🚀 💡 👉) and decorative emojis.

## Banned patterns (check by reading)
- **"It's not X. It's Y."** and "not just X, but Y" contrasts.
- **Rule of three everywhere.** Lists of exactly three adjectives or three short sentences in a row.
- **One-word dramatic lines.** "Simple." "Wild." "Huge." on their own line.
- **Rhetorical question, then the answer.** "The result? A 40% lift."
- **Colon setups.** "Here's what happened:" or "The lesson:" before the point.
- **Every line its own paragraph.** Broetry. Mix 1-line and 2 to 3 line paragraphs.
- **Ending on a question every single time.** Most posts end on a plain statement.
- **Perfectly parallel bullets.** Real notes are a bit uneven.
- **Summing up at the end** ("In short", "So,", "The takeaway:").
- **Title Case or bold in the middle of sentences** for emphasis.

## What human sounds like
- Contractions: I'm, didn't, it's, that's.
- First person, past tense for what happened: "I tried", "it broke", "I changed".
- Specifics only you'd know: the hex code, the 7°, the bug that took an evening.
- Admit something: what didn't work, what you're unsure about.
- Vary sentence length. Some long, some short. Not a rhythm.
- Plain verbs: made, built, tried, cut, moved, broke, fixed.
- Write it the way you'd say it out loud to a friend. If you wouldn't say it, cut it.
