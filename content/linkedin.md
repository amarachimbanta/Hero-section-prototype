# LinkedIn playbook

Skill: `/linkedin-post` · Drafts: `content/posts/linkedin/` · Voice: `content/voice.md`

## Audience
Founders, product designers, marketers at SaaS companies. They scroll at work and reward clear thinking with saves and comments.

## Format rules
- Hook: first line, under ~12 words. It has to survive the "…see more" cut.
- Short paragraphs (1 to 2 lines), white space between.
- 120 to 250 words. Carousel captions can be shorter.
- End on a plain statement most of the time. A question only when I genuinely want answers, and not more than 1 post in 3.
- Max 3 hashtags, at the bottom. Emojis: TODO (none / sparing / yes).
- No external link in the body (put it in the first comment).

## Visuals
- Screen recording (10 to 20s) of the prototype, or a 5 to 8 slide carousel (PDF).
- First frame/slide must make sense with no caption.
