# X playbook

## Audience
Designers, indie hackers, design engineers, founders. Faster, more casual, more visual than LinkedIn.

## Mix
- **Mostly design**: builds, redesigns, design opinions, design humor.
- **Sometimes human**: the job hunt, working life, being a designer in Nigeria, the honest stuff.
- Same rule as LinkedIn: every post gives something (a build, a lesson, a laugh). No asks.

## Format rules
- **Single post**: ≤ 280 characters. One sharp idea + a visual.
- **Thread**: 4 to 8 posts. Post 1 must stand alone and promise the payoff; number them `1/`, or not, TODO pick one.
- Lowercase/casual tone is fine. TODO: my preference.
- 0 to 1 hashtags. No hashtag walls.
- Links go in a reply, not the first post. **Exception:** when the link *is* the work (a live build with a good preview card), put it in the first post. Mikky's Lagos Life post did this and got 331K views.
- Last post of a thread: the takeaway + a soft CTA (follow / repost post 1).

## Visuals
- Video or GIF of the interaction > static screenshot. Under 30s, loops cleanly, no sound needed.
- Crop tight: the detail, not the whole page.

## Cadence
- 1 to 3 posts a day. TODO: best times.
- Repurpose: every LinkedIn post can become a single X post + the visual; a design project can become a thread.

## Formula 1: The unsolicited build (start here for design posts)
From Mikky Innovate's Lagos Life post: **331K views, 3.2K likes, 605 reposts, 278 replies.** The best way for a designer to go viral on X while looking like someone you want to hire.

1. **Tag the founder or brand, and say you're a fan.** "Hi @founder, I'm a designer and a big fan of [product]." Their audience sees it, they might reply or quote it, and that spreads it.
2. **Name one gap in one line, with a business reason.** "No page shows the game before you sign up, or shows brands how the ads work." That's sign-ups and ad revenue, said in plain words.
3. **"So I designed and built one."** Proof, not a pitch. A real, live page.
4. **Give it away.** "Unofficial, and it's yours." No ask, no "hire me". The generosity is what makes people repost.
5. **The live link with a great preview card is the visual.** The card (title, logo, a bit of personality) has to look good on its own, since that's what people see in the feed.
6. **Short.** About 45 words.

**Rules for doing it right**
- Pick a product I genuinely use and like, ideally one my audience knows (Nigerian, African or design-world brands).
- The gap must be real and visible on their current site or app. Screenshot it before building.
- Mark it as unofficial and a concept. Don't use their trademark in a way that looks like the real thing; don't collect real user data.
- Build it properly: it's my portfolio piece. Deploy it (Vercel, Netlify) and set the preview card (`og:title`, `og:image`).
- Pin it if it does well.

**Template**
```
Hi @[founder or brand], I'm a designer and a big fan of [product]

I noticed a gap: [the one thing missing, said the way a customer feels it]
So I designed and built one. Unofficial, and it's yours

[live link]
```
