## Post
I started building my portfolio in June.

Designers kept saying a portfolio takes forever. I didn't believe them. I do now.

My developer was busy, and I didn't want to wait any longer. So I took the bold step and built it myself.

It's live. Every page works, every link works, and I built all of it.

A few weeks ago I said I wanted a portfolio that isn't a roller coaster, where someone hiring finds what they need without riding through it. This is that portfolio.

Building it myself also showed me how much every design decision asks of the person who has to code it. I'll design differently because of that.

Link in the comments. What's the first thing you notice?
