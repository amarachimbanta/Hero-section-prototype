## Post
I got removed from a Slack group this week for putting myself out there.

Then my mum called. Right in the middle of my LinkedIn hour.

I was already in a bad mood. Setback after setback, and the Slack thing was still stinging. So I said what I always say.

"Mummy, let me call you later."

She didn't let it go this time.

"When are you going to talk to me? All the time, 'let me talk to you later.' And we are not even in the same environment."

That one landed.

I'd been giving my best hours to strangers on the internet. Posting, commenting, pitching, refreshing. And telling the people who actually care about me to wait.

After I called her back, one thing kept coming back to me.

No matter what happens out there, you can always go home.

The ghosting and the group that removed you don't change who's waiting for your call.

It's Friday. Call your mum back. Or whoever home is for you.
