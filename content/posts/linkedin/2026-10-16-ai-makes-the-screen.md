## Post
Anyone can generate a dashboard in 30 seconds now.

I use Claude and VS Code in my own work. I used them on the Nexera ERP. AI is very fast at screens.

But the result the founder cared about didn't come from a screen.

At Nexera, onboarding used to take hours. After the redesign it took under a minute, and activation went up 40%.

No tool gave us that. It came from UX research: understanding how people actually managed their inventory, then building the flow around that.

So if you're a founder hiring a designer right now, skip "can you use AI?" Everyone can.

Ask "what did you decide not to build, and why?"

The answer tells you if they'll grow your numbers or just fill your Figma file.

AI can make the screen. A designer decides which screen should exist.
