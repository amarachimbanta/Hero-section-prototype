## Post
Most App Store screenshots are a list of features. Nobody downloads a feature.

This week I designed the App Store screenshots for QuieTell, an app for cat owners.

People decide in the App Store in seconds, swiping sideways. So every screenshot had to answer a worry a cat owner already has, in their words, before they even look at the phone.

So instead of "Community", "Health log" and "Profiles", the headlines say:

"You're not the only one wondering"
"Every check-in, on record"
"Find your cat people"

The second one carries the most weight. Under it: "Show your vet exactly what shifted and when." That's the moment an owner pictures the app being useful in their real life.

The screen inside each frame is only there to prove the line above it.

If you have an app, read your screenshot headlines out loud. If they sound like your menu, rewrite them as the thing your user is already worried about.

Your screenshots are the landing page people swipe through right before they decide.
