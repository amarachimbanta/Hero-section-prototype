## Post
Yesterday I spent the whole day at someone else's convocation.

Her parents couldn't make it. Neither could her siblings. On one of the biggest days of her life, she was about to do it alone.

So I went.

I walked in with her. I stayed for the photo shoot. I made sure that on the day she finished, someone was there to see it.

It wasn't my day. It didn't move my portfolio, my job hunt or my follower count.

But that's part of building real relationships. Sometimes you show up when it costs you something and pays you nothing back.

You never know where anyone will be in a few years. They'll remember who stood next to them when it mattered.

Who showed up for you when nobody else could?
