## Post
Watch the green tile.

A button says "Pay €11.90". A receipt slides out. The button turns to "Paid".

Two seconds. No words. You already know what happened.

Most people treat animation as decoration. The good kind is information. It shows where something came from, what changed, and what happens next.

I built this whole hero around that idea. Every tile tells a tiny story before you read the headline. A profile badge hanging on its lanyard. A Liquidmetal button catching the light. A ring that pulses like it's waiting for you.

Static screens make people read. Moving ones make people get it.

Which tile did you watch first?
