## Post
In high school, I had to choose: science or art.

We were told something I only understood years later. Neither is more important. Science needs art, and art needs science.

Somewhere along the way, the tech industry forgot that.

Designers still get treated as the nice-to-have. The last hire. The lowest offer on the table.

Yet put two products side by side, one built with a designer and one without. You can tell which is which in seconds.

The confusing signup. The checkout people abandon. The app nobody opens twice.

Design is easy to overlook until it's missing. That's exactly why it gets underpaid.

What do you think keeps designers underpaid?
