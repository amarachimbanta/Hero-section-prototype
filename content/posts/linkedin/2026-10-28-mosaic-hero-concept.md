## Post
Mosaic's AI makes motion design from one sentence.

Their homepage doesn't move.

So I redesigned the hero to show the product instead of describing it. A visitor should see Motion working in the first 3 seconds, before reading a word.

What I changed:

1. One line that says what it does: "Type a sentence, get motion design."
2. A wall of real motion work playing on loop. A badge on a lanyard, a receipt printing out, a ring of light.
3. A tactile style. Glossy buttons and objects that look like you could touch them. Designers call it neo-skeuomorphism, and for a motion product it makes the page feel alive.

The business side: the hero is the first thing every visitor sees. When it shows the product working, more people get it in seconds, and more of them press "Try Motion".

Unofficial concept. I'm a fan of what Mosaic is building.

Flat or tactile: which one makes you click?
