## Post
One of my posts reached 15,631 people.

Another reached 749.

The smaller one taught me more.

It still got 31 comments. Real people, stopping to talk.

I used to judge every post by its impressions. Big number, good post. Small number, flop.

Then I looked at my post about keeping a relationship with a founder. 39 reactions. 42 comments. More people talked than liked.

Impressions tell you how many people saw you. Comments tell you how many people felt something.

So now I check a different number first.

What's the first number you check when you post?
