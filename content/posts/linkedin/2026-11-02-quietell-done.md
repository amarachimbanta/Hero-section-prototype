## Post
QuieTell is done. It took longer than I planned, and I'm glad it did.

It started with one problem. Cats hide it when something is wrong. Owners notice the small changes one at a time, never all together.

Then life got in the way. There were gaps. Weeks where the Figma file didn't move.

But every time I came back, I learned more about people than about cats.

People don't trust their memory when they're worried, so every check-in is kept on record for the vet.

People ask others before they act, so there's a community that says "You're not the only one wondering."

None of those decisions are about colours or fonts. They're about worry, memory and trust.

The full case study is on my Behance (link in the comments).

And I'm open to new product design roles. If you're building something where understanding people matters, my DMs are open.
