## Post
Picture a 68-year-old woman opening her medication app at 7am.

The screen is black. The text is thin and grey. She squints, holds the phone closer, then hands it to her grandson.

In the designer's Figma file, that app looked beautiful. Sleek, dark, modern.

It just wasn't built for her.

Dark mode is an option. It was never a requirement.

For a lot of people, especially with astigmatism or older eyes, light text on a dark background blurs. Health apps, banking apps, anything older users depend on every day often work better in light mode by default.

The fix is simple:

1. If your users skew older, start in light mode.
2. Make dark mode a setting they choose.
3. Test with real users, at the text size they'll actually read.

Trendy is easy to design. Readable is what keeps people coming back.

Have you ever used an app that looked great but was hard to read?
