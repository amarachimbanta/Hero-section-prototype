## Post
"Can we have it by Friday?"

It was Wednesday.

If you've worked in design, or anywhere really, you know this feeling.

Tight deadlines are stressful. But they've taught me something I'm grateful for.

When there's no time, you stop polishing the third version of a button. You fix the thing users will hit first. You decide what actually matters, because you have to.

The work isn't always prettier. It's usually clearer.

Time pressure is a terrible boss and a great editor.

What's the tightest deadline you've ever had, and did you make it?
