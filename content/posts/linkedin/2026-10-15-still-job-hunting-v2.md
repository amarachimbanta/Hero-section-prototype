## Post
i'm excited to announce i'm still job hunting.

June to September. Over 500 rejections. Shortlisted more times than I can count, then ghosted. Dozens of cold DMs and emails, and not a single reply.

The silence is louder than the rejections.

Somewhere in there I stopped waiting for the market and started building. I've been leaning into design engineering, so I design it and then I code it.

This is one of the things I shipped. A hero section where the dashboard runs itself. Every packet of light is a piece of work moving through the product, and the numbers really go up while you watch.

If you're hiring a product designer who can also build the thing, my DMs are open.
