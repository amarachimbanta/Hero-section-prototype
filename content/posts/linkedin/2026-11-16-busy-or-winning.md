## Post
$24,580 in revenue. 486 orders. 154 customers.

Most small businesses have numbers like these. Very few can see them in one place.

They live in a notebook, a POS export, three spreadsheets and the owner's memory.

So decisions get made on feel. Restock what sold last time. Add staff when it feels busy.

I designed The Style Room's sales overview to change that. Every number a store owner needs, on one screen, before the doors open.

Which day makes the most money. Which number quietly dropped.

A business can't fix what it can't see.

Where do your business numbers live right now?
