## Post
Tola used to count her sales in a notebook.

Every night after closing, she sat with a pile of receipts, trying to work out if it had been a good month.

She knew she was busy. She didn't know if she was winning.

Then she saw it all on one screen.

$24,580 in revenue. 486 orders. Saturdays brought in $5,100. Wednesdays barely made $1,850.

The denim jacket she almost stopped stocking was her best seller. 86 sold this month.

And one small red number: 1.2% fewer customers than last month.

For the first time, she wasn't guessing. She put more staff on Saturdays and started calling the customers who hadn't come back.

That's the kind of store owner I designed The Style Room's sales overview for. The one closing up at 9pm, tired, who just wants to know one thing: am I okay?

What would you want your numbers to tell you at the end of a long day?
