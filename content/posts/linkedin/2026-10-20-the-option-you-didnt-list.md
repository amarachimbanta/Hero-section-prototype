## Post
Every list of options leaves someone out.

I'm designing health check-ins for a cat app. Owners tap what they've noticed, like eating less or sleeping more.

But cats are cats. Sooner or later an owner sees something that isn't on my list.

So I added an assistant. It already knows the cat: what the owner has told us, her normal, her past check-ins. You describe what's going on in your own words and it helps you make sense of it.

The design calls I made:

1. Suggested questions, so nobody stares at an empty chat box.
2. A mic button, because a worried owner shouldn't have to type.
3. "I'm not a vet" at the top, before the first message. It explains the cat's record and sends anything medical to the vet.

Now the business side.

An owner who can't find their problem in a list usually closes the app. An owner who can describe it stays, and comes back. That's retention.

Every question typed in that chat also tells the team which option is missing from the check-in. Free product research, every day.

And a feature that knows your cat this well is an easy reason to pay for a premium plan.

Every question someone types is an option you forgot to design.
