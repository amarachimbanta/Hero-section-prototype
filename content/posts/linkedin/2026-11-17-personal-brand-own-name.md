## Post
In June, I started building my personal brand under a name I thought was clever.

Five months later, I'm rebranding.

It started with a message from someone who reached out with advice I didn't ask for, but needed.

They said: build it under your own name. This is a brand you could be using for 30 years. A made-up name can be taken, copied, or tangled up in a legal fight you never saw coming. Your own name is already yours.

I sat with that for a while.

Then I realised my first version still did its job. It got me started.

Nobody gets their brand right on day one. You post, you test, you cringe at old posts, and slowly it gets clearer. Clarity comes from doing the work.

What I keep coming back to is simple. A personal brand should feel personal. It's you, talking about your work, your wins and your bad days. Nobody else can be that.

So if you're waiting until it's perfect, start messy. You can rebrand as many times as you need to.

Did you build your brand under your own name, or something else?
