# Swipe file: LinkedIn posts that took off

The non-humor posts. Screenshots: `content/swipe/others/` (other people) and `content/swipe/mine/` (my own). Humor has its own file: `content/humor.md`.

Skill: `/swipe` logs screenshots and compares my posts with the winners. `/linkedin-post` uses the **Anatomy** and **Structures** below, even for design posts.

The point isn't to copy topics. A post about ADHD and a post about a hero section can use the same structure. Take the shape and fill it with my design work.

## Anatomy of a post that travels
Every logged post gets scored on these. Most winners hit at least 5.

| # | Part | What it does | Question to ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook with tension** | The first 1 to 2 lines set up a conflict or a surprise, before "see more" | Would I click "see more"? |
| 2 | **Who's talking** | A real person with a real situation (age, job, numbers) | Do I know who this is about in 2 lines? |
| 3 | **Proof** | Concrete, specific numbers or details that make it believable | Could someone check it? |
| 4 | **The turn** | The "but" where the post flips direction | Is there a moment where it changes? |
| 5 | **Relatable details** | Small, specific moments readers have lived | Will someone say "this is me"? |
| 6 | **Vulnerable bit** | Something slightly embarrassing or honest | Is there a risk in posting this? |
| 7 | **Reframe** | One line that changes how the reader sees it | Is there a line people would quote? |
| 8 | **Ending that asks something of the reader** | A question to themselves, or a small action | Does it end on them, not me? |
| 9 | **Visual** | Face photo, real screenshot, or work. Not stock | Would the image stop the scroll alone? |

## Structures that work
Reusable shapes. Add one each time a new winner shows a new shape.

### 1. Mirror lists (from the ADHD post)
```
Identity + tension hook (good at X, bad at Y)
"From the outside / At work, I:"  then a list of 3 to 4 wins with numbers
Short reaction line
"But" turn
"The second I [switch context], I:" then a list of 3 to 4 small fails, very specific
Not-asking-for-pity line, owning it
One quotable reframe line
Question the reader asks themselves
Stakes line (what changes if they get it)
```
**Design version to try** (needs my real details, don't invent them):
At work I can spot a 1px misalignment across the room, name a font from a blurry screenshot, and get a stakeholder to agree on a layout. On my own stuff I [real things: a portfolio not updated since X, a side project in its Nth version, a desktop with N screenshots]. Reframe: "Designing for other people is easier than designing for yourself because nobody is giving you a brief."

## Patterns across the winners
Updated once there are 5+ rows.

Early notes (1 post, not a pattern yet):
- Comments matter more than reactions. A post that makes people tell their own story spreads.
- Specific small details (3 days, 4 minutes, 4 days) beat big claims.
- The post is about the writer, but the ending turns it to the reader.
- Face photo on a personal post. Real work on a work post.
