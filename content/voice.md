# My voice (shared across X and LinkedIn)

> Platform rules live in `content/linkedin.md` and `content/x.md`. This file is *who I am* on both.
> Fill in the TODOs; paste real posts of yours, because that's what makes drafts sound like you.

## Tone
- Plain, confident, specific. A designer explaining work to a smart friend.
- Show the work, explain the decision, share what you learned.
- TODO: 3 words for how I want to sound (e.g. "curious, direct, generous")

## Always
- Concrete over abstract: name the element, the number, the trade-off.
- One idea per post.
- Every claim traceable to real work. Never invent metrics, clients or results.

## Words I avoid
See `content/human-writing.md`. It is a hard rule, not a suggestion.

## Content pillars
1. **Build log**: what I made and one decision behind it.
2. **Before and after**: a design change and why it's better.
3. **Teardown**: what a well-known product does well (or doesn't).
4. **Principle**: one design rule, proven with my own work.
5. **Process**: how I use Claude / Figma / code in my workflow.
